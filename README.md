                                                Maven Roasters Sales Analysis

**Overview**
This project analyzes transactional sales data from Maven Roasters, a fictional coffee shop chain operating in New York City. Using Tableau, the analysis examines revenue performance, customer purchasing patterns, product popularity, and store level trends across multiple locations.

The objective is to transform raw transaction data into actionable business intelligence that can support operational planning, inventory management, staffing decisions, and strategic growth initiatives. Through interactive dashboards and data storytelling, the project identifies key sales drivers, peak demand periods, and top performing products.

**Objective**
- Analyze sales performance across multiple store locations
- Identify revenue trends and seasonal patterns
- Evaluate product and category performance
- Understand customer purchasing behavior by day and hour
- Present findings through interactive Tableau dashboards and storytelling

**Key Insights**
- The company experienced its largest weekly sales decline between July 18 and July 25, 2023, with Hell's Kitchen accounting for the largest portion of the decrease.
- Coffee was the highest-performing product category, generating the most revenue across all three locations, followed by Tea and Bakery products.
- Sales were strongest during weekdays, particularly Monday through Friday, while weekend sales remained comparatively lower.
- Transaction analysis revealed that certain lower-revenue products, such as Flavors, generated relatively high transaction volumes, suggesting potential pricing optimization opportunities.
- Product-level analysis identified both top-performing and underperforming items, providing insight into which products may benefit from additional marketing, pricing adjustments, or potential menu optimization.
